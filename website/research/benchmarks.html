    Where we're going: the most reliable autonomous landing in the world.

    That is our goal, not a result. Below, each model shows what we measured, what our own training curves project for more training, and the target it still has to reach. Projections are curve fits to our logs, never measurements, and none is compared with a flown mission.

    Targets without a curve

    Where the training curve does not trend towards the goal, or no curve exists, we state a target and the reason we do not project.

    Roadmap

    1. More training. Finish the planned schedules in uninterrupted runs, starting with the light network's 6,000 steps, and score them on the same held-out windows.
    2. Fresh unseen maps. New sites that no model, rule or person has looked at, held back for one final scoring.
    3. Hazard on flat ground. Training data from flat sites, where the hazard network misses the most unsafe cells today.
    4. Light model fully trained. A complete training run, adopted only if it beats the frozen checkpoint by the pre-registered margin.
    5. Evolution swarm. Designed and throughput-tested, not yet run: a first real run with every generation logged.
    6. Flight-like 6-DOF simulation. Attitude dynamics, sensor and actuator models for the pilot, in place of today's 3-DOF point-mass descent simulation.
    7. Hardware-in-the-loop. The pilot and its classic backup running on flight-representative computers in a closed loop with the simulator.

    Method & data

    Benchmarks: assets/data/benchmarks.json, a copy of data/handoff_macmini_1115/benchmarks_final.json (benchmarks only; nothing trained, tuned or re-flown for it). Every row names its own source file above.

    Statistics: assets/data/stats.json and assets/data/csv/*.csv, written by website/tools/export_bench_csv.py from the hand-off records (exact McNemar p recomputed from discordant counts, Holm adjustment, bootstrap effect sizes, touchdown limits from config/mission.yaml, checkpoint sha256).

    Training record, projections and targets: assets/data/projections.json, a copy of data/handoff_macmini_1115/projections.json; each training run names its log file. Projected values are extrapolations of the logged Site 11 validation curves and are shown only next to the measured value.
